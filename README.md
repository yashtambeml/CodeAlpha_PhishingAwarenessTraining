This project is an educational module/presentation designed to raise awareness about phishing attacks and social engineering tactics used by cybercriminals. It helps users understand how attackers manipulate human behavior and provides practical strategies to stay safe online.

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Educate users about phishing attacks and social engineering tactics

- Provide best practices to avoid cyber threats  
- Include real-world examples for better understanding  
- Improve learning through interactive quizzes and engagement  

## 🧠 What is Phishing?  
Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, or banking details by pretending to be a trusted source.

## 🎭 Social Engineering Tactics Explained  
Attackers often exploit human psychology instead of technical vulnerabilities. Common tactics include:

- Urgency → “Your account will be locked immediately!”  
- Authority → Pretending to be a bank, company, or government  
- Fear → Threatening consequences to force quick action  
- Curiosity → “You won a prize! Click here”  
- Trust Exploitation → Impersonating known contacts  

## 🚨 How to Recognize Phishing Emails  
- Suspicious or unknown sender  
- Urgent or threatening language  
- Spelling and grammar mistakes  
- Unexpected attachments or links  
- Email addresses that don’t match official domains  

## 🌐 Fake Website Detection  
- Check URL carefully (e.g., amaz0n-login.com vs real domain)  
- Look for HTTPS and security certificate  
- Avoid entering sensitive data on unknown sites  
- Check website design inconsistencies  

## 🔍 Real-World Example  
Scenario:  
You receive an email saying:  
"Your bank account is locked. Click here to unlock."

You click the link  
A fake website opens  
You enter your login details  
Result: Your data gets stolen

## 🛡️ Best Practices to Stay Safe  
- Always verify the sender  
- Double-check URLs before clicking  
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)  
- Use strong and unique passwords  
- Keep software and antivirus updated  

## ❌ What NOT to Do  
- Don’t click unknown links  
- Don’t trust urgent messages blindly  
- Don’t share sensitive information  
- Don’t download attachments from unknown sources  

## 📊 Key Takeaways  
- Phishing attacks rely on human error  
- Awareness is the first line of defense  
- Always think before you click
